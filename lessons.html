Module 1 · Days 1–5 · Guide: Boss Up

Build: Business setup and compliance

Lay the foundation: choose a business idea that fits the DC market, pick a legal structure, and map every license, permit, and registration step, including the Clean Hands certificate.

Day 1Orientation and Entrepreneurial Foundations+

Wealth mindset and setting financial goals, Business finance and financial literacy. Reference guide: Boss Up.

Learning objectives+

The participant will be able to:

  1. Complete the pre-assessment and set up Canvas and email.
  2. Name the business skills you already have from work, family, and the informal economy.
  3. Write down three business ideas to test this week.
Class agenda · 3 hours+
  1. Open · 20 min. Welcome, introductions, group agreements, and what the next 20 days look like.
  2. Assessment · 30 min. Pre-assessment in Canvas (knowledge and confidence). Staff help anyone who needs it.
  3. Tech Catch-Up · 30 min. Log in to Canvas and email on the laptop and phone. Reset passwords, turn on two-step login.
  4. Expert Content · 60 min. Flipping the Game: from informal enterprise to formal business. The skills you already have (sales, negotiation, managing money and people) and how they transfer to a legal business.
  5. Build · 30 min. Business idea brainstorm: three ideas, who would pay, and why you.
  6. Close · 10 min. Exit ticket: the idea you are most excited about, and one support you need this month.
Readiness support+

Day one is about safety and belonging. Nobody is asked to share their story. The Case Management Associate meets each participant this week for a short readiness review (ID, phone, housing, supervision schedule).

Open the Readiness Review

In-class assignment+
  1. Build: Business idea brainstorm: three ideas, who would pay, and why you.
  2. Exit ticket: Which idea are you most excited about, and what is one support you need this month?

Answer the exit ticket in Canvas

  4. Build · 20 min. Pick your idea. Fill in the first half of the one-page plan.

Many participants come home to a different DC. The opportunity scan doubles as a tour of what changed.

Day 3Business Structures and Registering in DCTruist Bank+

Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices, Business banking and financial services, Business finance and financial literacy. Reference guide: Boss Up.

Learning objectives+

The participant will be able to:

  1. Compare sole proprietorship, LLC, partnership, and corporation.
  2. List the steps to register a business in DC, including an EIN and a Clean Hands certificate.
  3. Explain why business and personal money stay separate.
Class agenda · 3 hours+
  1. Open · 15 min. Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: making an account on a government website safely.
  2. Expert Content · 60 min. Business structures and risk. DC registration path: DLCP CorpOnline, IRS EIN, OTR tax registration, Clean Hands certificate, Basic Business License.
  3. Master Class · 60 min. Truist Bank, Bank On Your Success (session 1 of 4): business finance basics, business structure, and organizing your finances.
  4. Build · 35 min. Choose your structure and start your DC Compliance Checklist.
  5. Close · 10 min. Exit ticket.
  • Truist Bank, Bank On Your Success: Business finance basics; business structure and organizing your finances.
Readiness support+

Clean Hands: DC will not issue a business license to anyone who owes the District more than $100 in taxes, fines, or fees. Participants check their status on MyTax.DC.gov early, and case management helps set up payment plans. Child support arrears are handled through the Child Support Services Division.

Open the Readiness Review

In-class assignment+
  1. Build: Choose your structure and start your DC Compliance Checklist.
  2. Exit ticket: Which business structure fits your business, and why?

Day 4Licensing, Regulation, and Ethical BusinessMaster Class+

Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices. Reference guide: Boss Up.

Learning objectives+

The participant will be able to:

  1. Find the licenses and permits your business needs (for example, food, vending, home occupation, trades).
  2. Explain how a record can affect some licenses, and where to get help.
  3. Write three ethical rules your business will follow.
Class agenda · 3 hours+
  1. Open · 15 min. Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: reading a government PDF and filling in a form online.
  2. Expert Content · 60 min. Compliance with Licensing and Regulatory Standards, and Ethical Business Practices (Boss Up). Contracts, honest pricing, paying taxes, treating workers fairly.
  3. Master Class · 60 min. DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP) representative, plus a local entrepreneur on their real startup licensing story.
  4. Build · 35 min. Add every license and permit for your business to the checklist.
  5. Close · 10 min. Exit ticket: the licenses your business needs.
  • Master Class: DLCP representative and a local DC entrepreneur.
Readiness support+

Some occupational and business licenses ask about criminal records. The class covers which ones, what DC law allows licensing boards to consider, and where to get free legal help with record sealing (your instructor confirms current rules with you).

In-class assignment+
  1. Build: Add every license and permit for your business to the checklist.

Finance: Financial planning and profitability

Build a financially viable business. Strengthen personal financial management, then set prices, build a revenue model, and create startup and monthly budgets that a finance professional reviews.

Day 11Personal Financial Management and Credit+

Business finance and financial literacy, Wealth mindset and setting financial goals, Business banking and financial services. Reference guide: Money Moves.

Learning objectives+

The participant will be able to:

  1. Build a personal monthly budget.
  2. Pull your free credit report and read it.

Class agenda · 3 hours+
  1. Open · 15 min. Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: banking apps, Zelle, Cash App, and avoiding payment scams.
  2. Expert Content · 75 min. Financial Literacy (Money Moves): budgeting, credit scores, credit reports, secured cards, and second-chance bank accounts.

  4. Build · 20 min. Personal budget worksheet.
  5. Close · 10 min. Exit ticket.
  • Field Experience: Credit report lab.
Readiness support+

People returning after years away may have no credit history, old debts, or identity theft. Pulling a credit report is private and optional; case management helps dispute errors and find second-chance banking.
