Module 4 · Days 16–20 · 15 hours · Guide: Flipping the Game

LAUNCH

Prepare for launch. Plan staffing, use AI tools, prepare a resume and interview skills for income while you build, then pitch your business to a panel of business owners.

By the end of this module

  • Combine the business plan, brand, financial model, and marketing strategy into one capstone portfolio.
  • Write a resume and answer interview questions, including how to talk about time away, with confidence.
  • Deliver a formal pitch to a panel of business representatives.

Outcome: A launch-ready business validated by an external panel of business owners.

Module project

Capstone Business Portfolio + Final Pitch

  • Business setup plan (Module 1)
  • Brand kit (Module 2)
  • Financial plan (Module 3)
  • Marketing strategy
  • 90-day launch plan
  • Resume
  • 5-minute pitch with slides

Day 16 · 3 hoursStaffing Strategy and Artificial Intelligence in Business

Topics: Business finance and financial literacy, Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices

Today you will

  • Compare employees, contractors, and partners, and the rules for each.
  • Use AI tools for business tasks (drafting, planning, customer messages) safely.
  • Start the marketing strategy for the portfolio.

Field · AI business tools lab.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: trying an AI assistant on a phone.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Understanding Staffing Needs and Types; Leveraging Artificial Intelligence (Flipping the Game).
  3. 60 minField Experience AI lab: use AI to draft a marketing plan, a customer email, and pitch slide outlines, then fact-check and edit them.
  4. 35 minBuild Marketing strategy section of the capstone portfolio.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Reentry support. AI tools did not exist in this form a few years ago. The lab starts from zero and covers privacy and scams.

Day 17 · 3 hoursResume Development and Interview Preparation

Topics: Resumes and interviews, Public speaking and professional communication

Today you will

  • Write a resume that shows business and life skills.
  • Answer common interview questions, including questions about time away.
  • Know your rights under DC fair-chance hiring law.

Field · Mock interviews.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Resume and interview preparation: an entrepreneur's resume, filling gaps honestly, and the STAR method. Many owners keep a job while the business grows.
  3. 60 minField Experience Mock interviews with staff and partners, with written feedback.
  4. 35 minBuild Resume draft in Canvas; capability statement final version.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Reentry support. DC's Fair Criminal Record Screening law limits when most employers may ask about records (your instructor confirms current rules with you). Participants practice a short, honest, forward-looking answer about gaps.
Exit ticket
How will you answer 'Tell me about yourself'?

Day 20 · 3 hoursCapstone Pitch Competition

Topics: Public speaking and professional communication, Relationship building and networking

Today you will

  • Pitch your business to an external panel.
  • Complete the post-assessment.
  • Leave with a plan, a network, and your next steps.

Field · Pitch competition before an external panel.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Welcome guests, panel, families, and ONSE staff.
  2. 105 minCapstone Pitch competition: each participant delivers a 5-minute pitch plus panel questions, scored on viability, clarity, financial readiness, and market alignment.
  3. 25 minAssessment Post-assessment in Canvas and program evaluation survey.
  4. 35 minClose Awards (top three receive a $500 startup package), certificates, GWBCC membership activation, and next steps.
Reentry support. Graduates keep Canvas access, their reference guides, the GWBCC membership, and follow-up from case management after the program ends.
