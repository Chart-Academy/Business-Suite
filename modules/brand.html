Module 2 · Days 6–10 · 15 hours · Guide: Boss Up

BRAND

Find out what customers actually want before spending a dollar. Run customer interviews and a survey, then build a brand, a social media plan, and an elevator pitch on that evidence.

By the end of this module

  • Build a data-informed target audience profile from 1:1 customer interviews and a survey.
  • Develop a complete brand kit: name, tagline, logo, and market position.
  • Deliver a 60-second elevator pitch and introduce yourself professionally at a networking event.

Outcome: A customer-validated brand and market fit.

Module project

Brand Kit

  • Business name and tagline
  • Logo (draft)
  • Target audience profile from interviews and survey data
  • 30-day social media content plan
  • Capability statement (intro version)
  • Elevator pitch (60 seconds)

Day 6 · 3 hoursCustomer Discovery

Topics: Branding and marketing fundamentals, Public speaking and professional communication

Today you will

  • Write a customer interview script that avoids leading questions.
  • Practice interviews in pairs and start real interviews.
  • Describe a first-draft target customer.

Field · 1:1 customer interviews (goal: 5 by Day 10).

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: recording notes on your phone.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Customer discovery: the five questions that tell you if people will pay.
  3. 60 minField Experience Customer interviews: practice in pairs, then interview at least two real people (by phone, on site, or in the community).
  4. 35 minBuild Draft your target customer profile.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Reentry support. Approaching strangers can be hard after years away. Scripts and pair practice come first, and participants may start with people they already know.
Exit ticket
What surprised you in your first interviews?

Day 7 · 3 hoursMarket Research and Demand Validation

Topics: Branding and marketing fundamentals, Pricing strategies and revenue models

Today you will

  • Build a market research survey in Google Forms.
  • Ask about price, preferences, and buying habits.
  • Send the survey to at least 15 target customers.

Field · Market research survey sent to target customers (goal: 15 responses).

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: making a Google Form and sharing a link by text.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Market research basics: survey questions that give honest answers, and reading the results.
  3. 60 minField Experience Market research: build, test, and send your survey.
  4. 35 minBuild Brand identity worksheet: values, personality, and what makes you different.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Exit ticket

Day 8 · 3 hoursBrand Identity Development

Topics: Branding and marketing fundamentals, Protection of intellectual property and trademarks, Business banking and financial services

Today you will

  • Create a business name, tagline, and draft logo.
  • Check that the name is available as a trademark and a web domain.
  • Explain why business and personal accounts stay separate.

Master Class · SCORE mentors (mentor matching begins).Truist Bank · Separating business and personal finances; financial accounts and tools.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: using Canva on the laptop.
  2. 45 minExpert Content Branding fundamentals and IP and Trademark Basics (Boss Up): name, tagline, colors, logo, position statement, and checking the name is free to use.
  3. 60 minMaster Class Truist Bank, Bank On Your Success (session 2 of 4): separating business and personal finances, financial accounts and tools.
  4. 50 minBuild Mentorship session with SCORE mentors while you build your logo and brand kit in Canva.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.

Day 9 · 3 hoursSocial Media Marketing and Content Strategy

Topics: Social media marketing and content strategy, Branding and marketing fundamentals

Today you will

  • Choose the right platforms for your customer.
  • Build a 30-day content plan.
  • Protect your privacy and reputation online.

Master Class · DC Chamber of Commerce: local brand visibility.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: business accounts on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Google Business Profile.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Social Media Marketing Strategies (Boss Up): content types, posting schedule, simple metrics, and using AI to draft posts.
  3. 45 minMaster Class DC Chamber of Commerce speaker on local brand visibility.
  4. 50 minBuild Draft your 30-day content plan and your first three posts.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Reentry support. Social media has changed a lot. Participants under supervision check whether their conditions limit social media use before opening business accounts. The class covers scams, privacy settings, and keeping a business page separate from a personal one.

Day 10 · 3 hoursProfessional Communication and Networking

Topics: Public speaking and professional communication, Relationship building and networking

Today you will

  • Deliver a 60-second elevator pitch.
  • Tell your story on your own terms, including how (or whether) to talk about time away.
  • Attend a networking event and follow up professionally.

Field · Networking event (GWBCC or DC small business event).

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Review checkpoint: elevator pitch practice with peer feedback.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Public Speaking and Professional Communication; Networking and Relationship Building (Boss Up): introductions, business cards, follow-up messages, and the capability statement.
  3. 45 minField Experience Networking: attend a DC small business or Greater Washington Black Chamber of Commerce event (or a facilitated networking session in class if no event falls this week).
  4. 50 minBuild Finish and submit the Module 2 project: Brand Kit.
  5. 10 minClose Module 2 reflection in Canvas.
Reentry support. Your story, your terms: participants practice a short, forward-looking way to explain a gap if they choose, and learn they never have to disclose more than the law requires.
