Module 1 · Days 1–5 · 15 hours · Guide: Boss Up

BUILD

Lay the foundation: choose a business idea that fits the DC market, pick a legal structure, and map every license, permit, and registration step, including the Clean Hands certificate.

By the end of this module

  • Choose a business structure (sole proprietorship, LLC, or other) that fits the business model and risk.
  • Produce a complete DC compliance pathway: registration, EIN, Clean Hands, Basic Business License, and permits.
  • Explain the ethical practices and intellectual property protections the business will use.

Outcome: A legally structured, registration-ready business plan.

Module project

Business Plan Draft + DC Compliance Checklist

  • Business idea and the problem it solves
  • Chosen business structure and why
  • DC licenses, permits, and registration steps for this business
  • Regulatory considerations
  • Ethical guidelines the business will follow
  • How the business name will be protected

Day 1 · 3 hoursWelcome Home to Business

Topics: Wealth mindset and setting financial goals, Business finance and financial literacy

Today you will

  • Complete the pre-assessment and set up Canvas and email.
  • Name the business skills you already have from work, family, and the informal economy.
  • Write down three business ideas to test this week.

Agenda

  1. 20 minOpen Welcome, introductions, group agreements, and what the next 20 days look like.
  2. 30 minAssessment Pre-assessment in Canvas (knowledge and confidence). Staff help anyone who needs it.
  3. 30 minTech Catch-Up Log in to Canvas and email on the laptop and phone. Reset passwords, turn on two-step login.
  4. 60 minExpert Content Flipping the Game: from hustle to business. The skills you already have (sales, negotiation, managing money and people) and how they transfer to a legal business.
  5. 30 minBuild Business idea brainstorm: three ideas, who would pay, and why you.
  6. 10 minClose Exit ticket: the idea you are most excited about, and one support you need this month.
Coming home tip. Day one is about safety and belonging. Nobody is asked to share their story. The Case Management Associate meets each participant this week for a short reentry needs check (ID, phone, housing, supervision schedule).
Exit ticket
Which idea are you most excited about, and what is one support you need this month?

Day 2 · 3 hoursUnderstanding a Business Plan

Topics: Business finance and financial literacy, Branding and marketing fundamentals

Today you will

  • Explain the parts of a one-page business plan.
  • Match a business idea to real demand in DC (stadium, corridors, Ward 7 and 8 set-asides).
  • Choose one idea and start the business plan draft.

Field · Opportunity scan of one DC corridor or development project.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: saving and finding files in Google Drive.
  2. 75 minExpert Content Understanding a Business Plan (Boss Up): the one-page plan and the business model canvas. Where DC's growth is: hospitality, retail, events, home and building services.
  3. 60 minField Experience Opportunity scan: small groups research one DC corridor or upcoming project and list the goods and services it will need.
  4. 20 minBuild Pick your idea. Fill in the first half of the one-page plan.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Coming home tip. Many participants come home to a different DC. The opportunity scan doubles as a tour of what changed.
Exit ticket
Who is your customer, and what problem do you solve for them?

Day 3 · 3 hoursBusiness Structures and Registering in DC

Topics: Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices, Business banking and financial services, Business finance and financial literacy

Today you will

  • Compare sole proprietorship, LLC, partnership, and corporation.
  • List the steps to register a business in DC, including an EIN and a Clean Hands certificate.
  • Explain why business and personal money stay separate.

Truist Bank · Business finance basics; business structure and organizing your finances.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: making an account on a government website safely.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Business structures and risk. DC registration path: DLCP CorpOnline, IRS EIN, OTR tax registration, Clean Hands certificate, Basic Business License.
  3. 60 minMaster Class Truist Bank, Bank On Your Success (session 1 of 4): business finance basics, business structure, and organizing your finances.
  4. 35 minBuild Choose your structure and start your DC Compliance Checklist.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Coming home tip. Clean Hands: DC will not issue a business license to anyone who owes the District more than $100 in taxes, fines, or fees. Participants check their status on MyTax.DC.gov early, and case management helps set up payment plans. Child support arrears are handled through the Child Support Services Division.
Exit ticket
Which business structure fits your business, and why?

Day 4 · 3 hoursLicensing, Regulation, and Ethical Business

Topics: Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices

Today you will

  • Find the licenses and permits your business needs (for example, food, vending, home occupation, trades).
  • Explain how a record can affect some licenses, and where to get help.
  • Write three ethical rules your business will follow.

Master Class · DLCP representative and a local DC entrepreneur.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: reading a government PDF and filling in a form online.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Compliance with Licensing and Regulatory Standards, and Ethical Business Practices (Boss Up). Contracts, honest pricing, paying taxes, treating workers fairly.
  3. 60 minMaster Class DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP) representative, plus a local entrepreneur on their real startup licensing story.
  4. 35 minBuild Add every license and permit for your business to the checklist.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket: the licenses your business needs.
Coming home tip. Some occupational and business licenses ask about criminal records. The class covers which ones, what DC law allows licensing boards to consider, and where to get free legal help with record sealing (your instructor confirms current rules with you).
Exit ticket
List the licenses and permits your business needs.

Day 5 · 3 hoursProtecting Your Name and Ideas + Technical Assistance Lab

Topics: Protection of intellectual property and trademarks, Compliance with licensing and regulatory standards, and ethical business practices

Today you will

  • Explain trademarks, copyright, and trade names, and search the USPTO database.
  • Get one-on-one help from a DC small business technical assistance provider.
  • Submit the Module 1 project.

Field · Technical Assistance Lab: DC APEX Accelerator and DSLBD Small Biz Assist.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Review checkpoint: compliance checklist peer check.
  2. 45 minExpert Content Protection of Intellectual Property and Trademarks (Boss Up): searching USPTO and the DC trade name registry before you print anything.
  3. 60 minField Experience Technical Assistance Lab with DC APEX Accelerator and DSLBD Small Biz Assist (on site or at the MLK Library).
  4. 50 minBuild Finish and submit the Module 1 project: Business Plan Draft + DC Compliance Checklist.
  5. 10 minClose Module 1 reflection in Canvas.
Coming home tip. Staff walk anyone without a working email or phone number through getting one before the lab.
Exit ticket
What is the next registration step you will take, and when?
