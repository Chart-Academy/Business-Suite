Module 3 · Days 11–15 · 15 hours · Guide: Money Moves

FINANCE

Make the numbers work. Rebuild personal financial footing, then set prices, build a revenue model, and create startup and monthly budgets that a finance professional reviews.

By the end of this module

  • Apply cost-based and value-based pricing to set profitable price points.
  • Build a 12-month revenue model and defensible startup and monthly budgets in a spreadsheet.
  • Open or plan a business bank account and write a personal financial goal statement.

Outcome: A financially viable business model.

Module project

Revenue Model + Startup and Monthly Budget

  • Pricing for each product or service (cost-based and value-based)
  • Break-even point
  • 12-month revenue model in Excel or Google Sheets
  • Startup budget
  • Monthly operating budget
  • Funding plan
  • Personal financial goal statement

Day 11 · 3 hoursMoney Moves: Personal Financial Footing

Topics: Business finance and financial literacy, Wealth mindset and setting financial goals, Business banking and financial services

Today you will

  • Build a personal monthly budget.
  • Pull your free credit report and read it.
  • Make a plan for fines, fees, debts, and rebuilding credit.

Field · Credit report lab.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: banking apps, Zelle, Cash App, and avoiding payment scams.
  2. 75 minExpert Content Financial Literacy (Money Moves): budgeting, credit scores, credit reports, secured cards, and second-chance bank accounts.
  3. 60 minField Experience Credit check lab: pull your free report at AnnualCreditReport.com and mark anything to fix.
  4. 20 minBuild Personal budget worksheet.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Coming home tip. People returning after years away may have no credit history, old debts, or identity theft. Pulling a credit report is private and optional; case management helps dispute errors and find second-chance banking.

Day 12 · 3 hoursBusiness Finance: Costs and Cash Flow

Topics: Business finance and financial literacy

Today you will

  • Separate fixed and variable costs.
  • Explain cash flow and how a business owner pays themselves.
  • Start a startup budget in a spreadsheet.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Tech Catch-Up: spreadsheet basics (cells, sums, and copying a formula).
  2. 75 minExpert Content Business Finance (Money Moves): startup costs, fixed and variable costs, cash flow, profit, and taxes to set aside.
  3. 60 minField Experience Spreadsheet lab: build your startup budget in Excel or Google Sheets.
  4. 20 minBuild List every monthly cost for your business.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Coming home tip. Spreadsheets may be new. Staff pair participants and use a fill-in template so nobody starts from a blank sheet.

Day 13 · 3 hoursPricing Strategies and Revenue Models

Topics: Pricing strategies and revenue models, Business finance and financial literacy

Today you will

  • Set prices using cost-based and value-based methods.
  • Find your break-even point.

Master Class · Selling the vision: communicating price and value.

  4. 50 minBuild Revenue model in the spreadsheet using your survey's price data.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.

Day 14 · 3 hoursFunding Your Business

Topics: Business finance and financial literacy, Business banking and financial services

Today you will

  • Compare savings, microloans, grants, and community lenders.
  • Explain what lenders look for.
  • Create a business borrowing profile.

Truist Bank · Borrowing for small business; where to find funding; what lenders look for; your borrowing profile.

Agenda

  1. 15 minOpen Check-in. Draft budget review with feedback.
  2. 60 minExpert Content Funding Your Business (Money Moves): DC grants and programs (DSLBD), CDFIs, microloans, and avoiding predatory lenders.
  3. 60 minMaster Class Truist Bank, Bank On Your Success (session 3 of 4): borrowing for small business, where to find funding, what lenders look for, and creating your borrowing profile.
  4. 35 minBuild Funding plan: how you will pay for your startup budget.
  5. 10 minClose Exit ticket.
Coming home tip. Case management connects participants to opening a business bank account with Truist or another partner.
