Day 1

Getting Started

Everything you need for your first day, and who to ask when something gets in the way.

Bring on Day 1

  • A government photo ID (or a plan with your case manager to get one)
  • Your phone, charged, with a working number
  • An email address you can log in to (we will help you make one)
  • Your supervision check-in schedule, if you have one, so we can plan around it
  • One or two business ideas, even rough ones

Missing something? Come anyway. Your case manager will help you sort it out.

5 minutes a day

Tech Catch-Up

Most days start with one quick digital skill. If you have been away for years, this is where you catch up. If you already know it, you help someone who does not.

  1. Logging in to Canvas and email; strong passwords and two-step login
  2. Saving, finding, and sharing files in Google Drive
  3. Government websites: making an account safely, reading PDFs, and filling in forms
  4. Recording notes and voice memos on your phone
  5. Google Forms for surveys; sharing a link by text
  6. Canva for logos and flyers
  7. Business social media accounts and privacy settings
  8. Banking apps, Zelle and Cash App, and spotting payment scams
  9. Spreadsheet basics: cells, sums, and formulas
  10. AI assistants: what they are good at, and what to check yourself

Finishing the program

To complete the program, attend at least 48 of the 60 hours, submit the three module projects, and pitch on Day 20. The top three pitches win a $500 startup package.
