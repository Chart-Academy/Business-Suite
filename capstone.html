Days 19 and 20 · two parts

Capstone Pitch Competition

Every participant presents their business to a panel of established business owners and professionals. The capstone has two parts: the Business Portfolio (submitted in Canvas by Day 19) and the Pitch (Day 20).

Part A: Business Portfolio (due Day 19)

Your portfolio brings together everything you built in the four modules. It must be consistent: the prices in your financial plan should match your pitch, and your brand should match your marketing.

  1. Business setup plan and DC compliance checklist
  2. Brand kit
  3. Financial plan: pricing, revenue model, budgets
  4. Marketing strategy and 30-day content plan
  5. 90-day launch plan
  6. Resume

Upload your portfolio in Canvas

Capstone

Part B: The Pitch (Day 20)

You present your business to a panel of established business owners and professionals, with ONSE staff, partners, and guests in the room.

  1. 5 minutes, with up to 8 slides
  2. The problem and your customer
  3. Your business and why you
  4. Evidence from interviews and the survey
  5. Price, revenue, and what it costs to start
  6. Your ask: what you need next
  7. 3 to 5 minutes of panel questions

The top three pitches receive a $500 startup package for licensing, incorporation, and launch.

In your own words · write this part yourself

  • “The problem my business solves is … for …”
  • “My next three steps to launch are …”
  • “I know people will pay because … (what my interviews and survey showed)”

How the panel scores you
