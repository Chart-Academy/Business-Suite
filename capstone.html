Days 19 and 20 · 50 points

Capstone: Pitch Day

Every participant presents their business to a panel of established business owners and professionals. The capstone has two parts: the Business Portfolio (submitted in Canvas by Day 19) and the Pitch (Day 20).

Part 1 · due Day 19

Business Portfolio

  • Business setup plan and DC compliance checklist
  • Brand kit
  • Financial plan: pricing, revenue model, budgets
  • Marketing strategy and 30-day content plan
  • 90-day launch plan
  • Resume
Upload in Canvas ↗

Part 2 · Day 20

The Pitch

  • 5 minutes, with up to 8 slides
  • The problem and your customer
  • Your business and why you
  • Evidence from interviews and the survey
  • Price, revenue, and what it costs to start
  • Your ask: what you need next
  • 3 to 5 minutes of panel questions

How the panel scores you

Viability · 10The business solves a real problem for a defined customer, backed by interview and survey evidence.
Financial readiness · 10Prices, revenue model, budgets, and funding plan are complete and realistic.
Market alignment · 10The business fits a real DC market opportunity and has a clear marketing strategy.
Clarity and delivery · 10The pitch is clear, on time, and confident, and the answers to questions are direct.
Portfolio completeness · 10All six portfolio pieces are submitted and consistent with each other.
The top three pitches receive a $500 startup package for licensing, incorporation, and launch.
Practice out loud
  • “The problem I solve is … for …”
  • “I know people will pay because … (what my interviews and survey showed).”
  • “It costs … to start, I charge …, and I break even after … sales.”
  • “What I need next is …”
