Business Suite Curriculum

The Entrepreneurship and Business Ownership Program: four weeks of in-person instruction that take you from a business idea to a registered, branded, and financially planned business, ending with a pitch to a panel of DC business owners. No business experience needed.

Start here → See the 20 lessons

For every participant: starting fresh, formalizing, or growing.

Who it's for

Who this course is for

Wherever you're starting from, the goal is the same: a legal, registered business you understand inside and out.

Path A

Starting from an idea

Test whether people will pay for your idea before you spend money, then turn it into a registered business with a plan, a brand, and a budget.

Path B

Formalizing work you already do

Already earning from a skill, a trade, or side work? Register it, price it properly, open a business bank account, and grow it on solid ground.

Path C

Building after time away

Catch up on the tools and systems that changed, clear the steps that come first (ID, Clean Hands, credit), and build something that is yours.

Outcomes

What you'll leave with

  • A launch-ready business portfolio: business plan, DC compliance checklist, brand kit, financial plan, and marketing strategy.

  • A registration path for your business: the structure, licenses, permits, and Clean Hands steps you need in DC.

  • Prices and budgets you can defend, reviewed by finance professionals.

  • A pitch delivered to real business owners, and a resume and interview practice for income while you build.

  • A professional network: a one-year Greater Washington Black Chamber of Commerce membership, SCORE mentors, and a Truist Bank banking relationship.

  • Tools to keep: a laptop, professional bag, Dress for Success package, your IRSJA guides, and Canvas access after you graduate.

Course plan

Four modules, 20 days, one business

Every class follows the same rhythm: Learn → Do → Build. You learn a skill, use it in the real market or with a guest expert, then add it to your own business portfolio. 60 hours, 3 hours a day.
