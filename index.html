ONSE Pathways · 60 hours · 20 days

Entrepreneurship and Business Ownership Program

Develop the skills, plans, and professional network to launch and operate a legally registered business in the District of Columbia. In four weeks you will build a business plan, a brand, a financial plan, and a pitch, and present it to a panel of DC business owners.

Curriculum by IRSJA · Delivered by Chart Academy

Four modules · one business

Build → Brand → Finance → Launch

Module 1 · Days 1–5

BUILD

Business setup and compliance. Lay the foundation: choose a business idea that fits the DC market, pick a legal structure, and map every license, permit, and registration step, including the Clean Hands certificate.

You build: Business Plan Draft + DC Compliance Checklist

Open module
Module 2 · Days 6–10

BRAND

Branding and market validation. Find out what customers actually want before spending a dollar. Run customer interviews and a survey, then build a brand, a social media plan, and an elevator pitch on that evidence.

You build: Brand Kit

Open module
Module 3 · Days 11–15

FINANCE

Financial planning and profitability. Build a financially viable business. Strengthen personal financial management, then set prices, build a revenue model, and create startup and monthly budgets that a finance professional reviews.

You build: Revenue Model + Startup and Monthly Budget

Open module
Module 4 · Days 16–20

LAUNCH

Execution and presentation. Prepare for launch. Plan staffing, use AI tools, prepare a resume and interview skills for income while you build, then pitch your business to a panel of business owners.

You build: Capstone Business Portfolio + Final Pitch

Every day · 3 hours

How each day works

  1. OpenCheck in, set the day's goal, and a 5-minute Tech Catch-Up.
  2. LearnExpert Content from your instructor and your IRSJA guide.
  3. DoField Experience in the real market, or a Master Class with a guest expert.
  4. BuildAdd today's piece to your business portfolio, then the exit ticket in Canvas.

What you leave with

Program resources and network

  • A launch-ready business portfolio: plan, brand, finances, and pitch
  • Your IRSJA guides to keep: Boss Up, Money Moves, Flipping the Game
  • A laptop, a professional bag, and a Dress for Success package
  • A one-year Greater Washington Black Chamber of Commerce membership
  • A business banking relationship through Truist Bank
  • Canvas access, templates, and resources after you graduate

Reentry support

Designed for returning citizens

If you have been away for a while, a lot changed: phones, apps, banking, how businesses get customers. This course meets you where you are. Every day starts with a short Tech Catch-Up, a case manager helps with ID, Clean Hands, supervision schedules, and anything else in the way, and nobody has to share their story unless they want to.
