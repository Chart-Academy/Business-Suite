How work is scored

How each project is scored

Each module project is worth 20 points: four rubric rows, each scored Full (5), Partial (3), or Not yet (1). The capstone is worth 50 points and is scored by the pitch panel and your instructor. Open a project to see its rubric.

How your work is reviewed

Your instructor scores every project with its rubric and leaves written feedback in Canvas within two class days.

  • Submit your work with the Submit in Canvas button in each lesson.
  • If a Google link isn't shared as Anyone with the link → Viewer, your work can't be read until you share it.
  • The daily exit ticket is how attendance is recorded, so answer it before you leave.

Pre- and post-assessment

You take the same short assessment twice: on Day 1 and on Day 20.

  • Day 1 isn't graded. It only shows where you're starting.
  • Day 20 shows your growth. Your results are shared with you and the program.

Finish strong

Certificate of completion

Complete the program to earn a Certificate of Completion and a digital badge for your LinkedIn profile.

  • Attend at least 48 of the 60 hours. Your daily exit ticket records attendance.
  • Submit all three module projects in Canvas.
  • Deliver your pitch at the Capstone Pitch Competition.
  • Show it off. List the certificate on your resume and capability statement, and add the badge to LinkedIn.
